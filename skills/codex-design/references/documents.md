# Documents, resumes, fliers, and brochures

Use `document.js` and `<doc-page size="a4|letter" orientation="portrait|landscape">`. Choose flowing content for multi-page documents and deliberate fixed composition for fliers or one-page resumes. The starter establishes paper geometry and print margins.

Use clear headings, readable body type, restrained emphasis, and enough white space. Preserve real names, facts, dates, and links supplied by the user; do not invent qualifications or citations. Keep sections together when feasible using print break rules. Verify page count and clipping in an actual PDF export.

A trifold uses three equal panels per side. Lay out the outside panels in fold order and the inside in reading order, with gutters and a clearly identified fold direction. Verify both sides at the requested paper size.

For actual Word, LaTeX, or spreadsheet output, use the corresponding available Codex artifact skill. This recipe creates printable HTML, not those file formats.
