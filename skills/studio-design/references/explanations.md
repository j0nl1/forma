# Explanation preset

Use this preset when the listener wants to understand a concept, mechanism or source. Follow the [shared source-to-audio workflow](source-to-audio.md) for evidence, timing, voices, reuse and assembly. Default to one narrator unless the user requests another format.

## Editorial direction

Establish what the listener will understand and how much background they have. Give necessary context, define unfamiliar terms, explain the mechanism, use a source-supported example or explicitly hypothetical analogy, and recap the conclusion. This is a flexible progression, not a mandatory section count.

Prefer concrete sentences and introduce prerequisites before dependent ideas. Explain numbers and comparisons in a form that can be followed without seeing a table. State limitations where they change the explanation. Distinguish a source's claims from your interpretation.

Use automatic duration to recommend the shortest useful explanation of the selected concepts. For a requested time, preserve the mechanism and essential qualifications before adding secondary detail. Do not manufacture a conversation, step-by-step procedure or filler to occupy the time.

## Rendering and review

Use coherent paragraphs as candidate blocks within the selected voice tool's limits, with stable narrator and source references. Keep pronunciation notes outside spoken text. Derive the voice input and transcript from the same script.

Check whether a listener can explain the main mechanism afterward, whether terms arrive before they are needed, and whether the example preserves the source's meaning. Review pronunciation and measured duration separately from factual accuracy. Use the shared helper for local measurement and assembly where its clip contract fits.
