# Summary preset

Use this preset for an audio summary or briefing whose purpose is to convey essential information quickly. Follow the [shared source-to-audio workflow](source-to-audio.md). Default to one narrator and adjust audience, tone, language and duration from the user's brief.

## Editorial direction

Establish which sources and question the summary covers. Lead with the central finding or argument, then include the most important supporting findings, constraints and unresolved points. End with a concise synthesis when it adds value.

Prioritize by relevance to the listener, not by giving every source section equal time. Preserve important numbers, units, qualifications and disagreements. Attribute disputed or source-specific assertions. Do not merge conflicting sources into an invented consensus.

Explain only the background needed to understand the findings. A summary does not need a tutorial, detailed mechanism, artificial banter or a call to action. Mention consequential omissions when the selected scope or time excludes important material.

## Rendering and review

Keep each source-supported point as a coherent script block. Reuse the same evidence map and local production steps as other presets. Recommended duration follows the number and complexity of essential findings rather than source page count.

Review whether the summary preserves the material's central message and uncertainty, whether priorities match the brief, and whether shortened wording changes meaning. Check measured duration and delivery independently. Regenerate only revised blocks.
